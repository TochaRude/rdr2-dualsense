[size=5][b]DualSense for RDR2 (v2.0.0 — Community Edition)[/b][/size]

[b]Full PlayStation DualSense support for Red Dead Redemption 2 on PC — over USB or Bluetooth.[/b]
Originally created by [b]OldKrab[/b] ([url=https://www.nexusmods.com/reddeadredemption2/mods/11444]original Nexus Mods page[/url]) and continued independently with OldKrab's blessing by [b]TochaRude[/b].

Everything works straight from what is actually happening in the game — every gun has its own trigger feel, footsteps and hooves match the ground under you, and you can tweak every single setting inside the pause menu: [b]Pause > Settings > Controls > Controller > DualSense[/b].

[size=4][b]What's New Compared to OldKrab's v1.3.0[/b][/size]
If you played the original 1.3.0 release, here is everything new for players in v2.0.0:

[list]
[*][b]Huge In-Game Settings Menu in 8 Languages:[/b] The in-game [i]DualSense[/i] page grew from 27 to 63 options and automatically appears in [b]English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, or Russian[/b] (falling back to English for other languages). Includes a brand-new global [b]Trigger Strength[/b] slider (0–200%).
[*][b]Customizable Touchpad Gestures:[/b] Map [i]Click, Swipe Up/Down/Left/Right[/i], and [i]Two-Finger Tap[/i] directly in the pause menu to handy shortcuts like Map/Camera, Whistle, Weapon Wheel, Eagle Eye, Bag, Pause, and more.
[*][b]Bow, Lasso & Full-Travel Guns:[/b]
  [list]
  [*][b]Bow:[/b] R2 gets progressively heavier the further you draw, the wood creaks as it bends, your arm strains if you hold too long, and letting down feels different from loosing an arrow.
  [*][b]Rifles, Repeaters & Shotguns:[/b] Full-travel cycling on lever-action, bolt-action, and pump-action weapons with a mid-stroke click, dirt stiffness, and occasional dirty-gun slips.
  [*][b]All 93 Weapons Covered:[/b] Includes unique Story revolvers/pistols (like Midnight's & Granger's), DLC/Online variants, knives, axes, machetes, lanterns, bolas, and more.
  [*][b]Lasso & Fishing:[/b] Both L2 and R2 tighten while holding someone on the lasso; casting a fishing line loads progressively onto R2.
  [/list]
[*][b]Quick-Draw & Melee Feedback:[/b] Slowly half-pulling R2 while holstered on foot builds smooth duel tension up to a crisp wall; punches, blocks, and knockouts vibrate in the triggers (while Circle stays completely loose for melee).

  [list]
  [*][b]Eagle Eye & Lit Fuses:[/b] Soft focus chime with an icy blue-white glow for Eagle Eye, plus sparkling fuse crackles and an orange-to-red flickering lightbar when holding lit dynamite or fire bottles.

  [*][b]Expanded Weather:[/b] Hail, storm/blizzard wind gusts, and distant thunder rumbles alongside raindrops on Arthur's hat.
  [/list]
[*][b]Player LEDs & Authentic Controller Speaker Sounds:[/b] The 5 LEDs under the touchpad show remaining ammo, flash your health when it changes, or sweep like police lights when wanted. [*][b]Reliability & Polish:[/b] Clean controller shutdown when exiting RDR2 (lightbar & LEDs turn off instead of staying lit), crash-free ScriptHookRDR2 V2 save/load reloads, drift-free gyro aiming, relaxed silent triggers in menus and loading screens, and built-in PlayStation button prompts.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Core Features[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]Adaptive Triggers[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every weapon has its own take-up, wall, and crisp break — the shot fires right on the break
[*]Manual single-action cocking / cycling on R2 (aimed single-handed & longarms), with automatic cycling when hip-firing or dual-wielding
[*]Dirty guns feel stiff and rough (and can slip); cold and exhaustion add trigger tremor; stronger ammo kicks harder
[*]Dead Eye ticks on every painted target and kicks on every shot of the volley
[*]Fishing carries real line tension on L2 (straining before the line snaps) and cast weight on R2
[*]Quick-Draw R2 duel tension when holstered on foot; weapon wheel clicks like a selector switch
[/list]

[size=3][b]Voice-Coil Haptics[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Real hooves on 9 distinct surfaces (mud, gravel, wood, grass, snow, water, sand...) + Arthur's real footsteps & landings
[*]Directional bullet impacts, nearby gunfire, low-health & wanted heartbeat, shivering cold, explosions, rain/hail/storms/thunder
[*]Boat rowing & waves, passing trains, spooked horses, wagon ruts & braking, camp chores, cleaning guns, brushing horses, skinning, bathing, herb plucking, and table games (poker, blackjack, dominoes, five finger fillet)
[*]Gunshots shaped from the game's own sound recordings (also works with weapon sound mods)
[/list]

[size=3][b]Lightbar, LEDs, Speaker & Touchpad[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Lightbar reacts to gunfire flashes, hits, Dead Eye, Eagle Eye, lit fuses, lanterns, wanted police lights, low health, honor changes, and time of day — and turns cleanly off when exiting the game
[*]5 Player LEDs show clip ammo, health updates, or wanted sweeps
[*]Controller speaker (off by default, USB only) plays genuine RDR2 holster, lasso, bow, reload, blade, and Dead Eye sounds
[*]Touchpad Click, 4 Swipes, and Two-Finger Tap can be freely assigned in the pause menu
[/list]

[size=3][b]In-Game Settings & PlayStation Prompts[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]63 settings organized into 7 categories right in [b]Pause > Settings > Controls > Controller > DualSense[/b] (EN, DE, FR, ES, IT, PT, PL, RU, plus English fallback)
[*]PlayStation button icons & controller graphic included using Rockstar's own art (no extra PS icon mod required)
[/list]

[size=3][b]Dialogue Haptics (Experimental) & Microphone (Beta)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Dialogue Haptics[/b] (off by default): Feel every spoken line in your hands from the speaker's direction in cutscenes or across the world, with adjustable TV/headphone delay sync
[*][b]Microphone[/b] (off by default): Whistle into the controller mic to call your horse, or use voice commands ([i]"chao"[/i] to greet, [i]"easy"[/i] to calm your horse)
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/]Script Hook RDR2[/url] + an ASI loader ([code]dinput8.dll[/code] or [code]version.dll[/code])
[*]A DualSense controller via USB cable or Bluetooth (over Bluetooth everything works except the controller's built-in speaker and mic)
[*]Close DSX / DS4Windows and disable [b]Steam Input[/b] for RDR2 ([i]Steam > RDR2 > Properties > Controller > Disable Steam Input[/i])
[*]Remove other DualSense mods (e.g., DualSenseHaptics.asi, NodixDualSense.asi) and other PlayStation icon mods that replace [code]startup_data.rpf[/code]
[*][b]Lenny's Mod Loader (LML)[/b] for the in-game Settings menu and PlayStation button prompts (without LML, everything else still works via [code]DualSenseRDR2.ini[/code])
[*]Story mode only — move mod files out before playing Red Dead Online
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation & Updating[/b][/size]
Extract everything from the zip directly into your main RDR2 folder (where [code]RDR2.exe[/code] is located):
[code]DualSenseRDR2.asi
DualSenseRDR2\   (DualSenseCore.dll, weapons.json, haptics.json, translations.json, etc.)
lml\             (merges into your LML folder: lml\DualSenseRDR2 and lml\replace\...)[/code]
To update from an older version, simply overwrite the files — your [code]DualSenseRDR2.ini[/code] is preserved. Press [b]F9[/b] or hold the PS button for 1 second to reload settings in-game.
