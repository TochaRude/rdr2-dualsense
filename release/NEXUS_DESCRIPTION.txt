--- DESCRIPTION ---

[size=5][b]DualSense for RDR2 - Reworked (v2.0.0)[/b][/size]

[b]Complete PlayStation DualSense support for Red Dead Redemption 2 on PC — over USB or Bluetooth.[/b]
Originally created by [b]OldKrab[/b] ([url=https://www.nexusmods.com/reddeadredemption2/mods/11444]original Nexus Mods page[/url]) and continued independently with OldKrab's blessing by [b]TochaRude[/b].

Everything is driven live by what is happening in the game: every weapon has its own trigger pull, footsteps and hooves match the ground under you, and you can adjust all [b]63 settings[/b] directly in the pause menu under [b]Pause > Settings > Controls > Controller > DualSense[/b].

---

[size=4][b]Everything This Mod Adds to RDR2[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]Adaptive Triggers (93 Weapons & Gameplay)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Unique Trigger Feel per Weapon:[/b] Every gun has its own take-up, wall, crisp break, and recoil — the shot fires right on the break (covering all 93 Story & DLC/Online weapons, including unique revolvers & pistols). Automatic break alignment learns where your controller's R2 really fires.
[*][b]Realistic Single-Action, Lever, Bolt & Pump Handling:[/b] Revolvers, lever-action repeaters, bolt-action rifles, and pump shotguns are cocked or cycled with a full R2 stroke and mid-stroke click when aiming one weapon — while hip-fire fanning and dual-wielding cycle smoothly for you.
[*][b]Weapon Condition, Ammo & Fatigue:[/b] Clean guns pull like clockwork, dirty guns feel stiff and gritty (and can slip before cocking), stronger ammo (Express / High Velocity / Explosive) kicks harder, and exhaustion or freezing cold makes the trigger tremble. Pulling R2 on an empty chamber gives a crisp dry-fire click.
[*][b]L2 Aim Resistance & Quick-Draw:[/b] Optional L2 aim tension (Off, Light, or Full). Slowly half-pulling R2 while holstered on foot builds smooth Quick-Draw duel tension up to a crisp wall.
[*][b]Dead Eye:[/b] Every painted target ticks under your finger, and each shot of the volley kicks as it actually fires.
[*][b]Bow:[/b] R2 grows progressively heavier the further you draw, the wood creaks as it bends, your arm strains if you hold too long, and letting down early feels distinct from loosing the arrow.
[*][b]Lasso & Fishing:[/b] Both L2 and R2 stiffen while holding someone on the lasso with rope jerks and struggle. Casting a fishing line loads progressively onto R2 and tugs on a bite, while L2 carries real line tension and strains right before the line snaps.
[*][b]Melee & Weapon Wheel:[/b] Punches, blocks, and knockout finishers vibrate in the triggers (while Circle stays completely loose for melee). On the weapon wheel, L2 and R2 click like a mechanical selector switch as you browse.
[*][b]Unarmed Horse Gait & Global Strength:[/b] Optional subtle riding gait pulse in the triggers when unarmed on horseback, plus a single global [b]Trigger Strength[/b] slider (0–200%) in the pause menu.
[/list]

[size=3][b]HD Voice-Coil Haptics (USB & Bluetooth)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Footsteps, Hooves & 9 Ground Surfaces:[/b] Real footsteps and hoofbeats at every gait matching the actual surface (mud, gravel, wood, grass, snow, sand, stone, dirt, water) — sensitive enough for slow camp walks and noticeably heavier when sprinting or carrying a body.
[*][b]Water, Swimming & Bushes:[/b] Wading drags harder the deeper and faster you move; swimming transmits every left/right arm pull, kick, and horse paddling stroke plus water splashes; bushes and tall grass rustle around you as you push through.
[*][b]Horses, Boats, Wagons & Trains:[/b] Heavy thuds when mounting/dismounting a horse or wagon and on jump landings, nervous rein tugs + faster heartbeat when your horse is spooked, directional boat oar strokes & gentle hull waves, wagon ruts & surface-aware braking judder, and ground rumble + front/rear rail clacks near or inside moving trains.
[*][b]Combat, Explosions & Fight Focus:[/b] Directional bullet/melee/animal hits, fine hit-confirmation ticks, directional nearby gunfire pressure waves, authentic explosions with shockwaves shaking both triggers at close range, and fight-focus ducking so shots and hits always stand out clearly over background textures.
[*][b]Real Game Sound Gunshots:[/b] Weapon recoil and shot haptics are shaped straight from the game's own sound recordings — and automatically adapt if you use gun sound mods.
[*][b]Weather & World Atmosphere:[/b] Raindrops and hail pellets on Arthur's hat, howling storm/blizzard wind gusts, and distant rolling thunder; shivering in freezing cold; low-health and wanted heartbeats; rising/falling honor feedback; and full haptic support during cutscenes.
[*][b]Camp Life & Mini-Games:[/b] Bites, sips and puffs, progressive gun cleaning (starts gritty on dirty barrels and turns silky smooth with a polish click as it gets clean), brushing & feeding your horse, skinning, stowing pelts, multi-stage herb plucking (stem snap + root pull), bathing, crafting, cooking, and table games (five finger fillet, poker, blackjack, dominoes).
[*][b]Optional Game Audio Layer:[/b] Gently turn everything you hear in the game into stereo haptics in your hands (off by default, adjustable in the pause menu).
[/list]

[size=3][b]Lightbar, Player LEDs, Controller Speaker & Touchpad[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Reactive Lightbar:[/b] Glows and flashes for muzzle fire, hits, Dead Eye (sepia), Eagle Eye (icy blue-white), lit dynamite & fire-bottle fuses (orange-to-red flicker + sparkling grip crackle), carried lanterns, wanted police lights, low health, honor changes, low-battery warnings, and smooth time-of-day ambience. Turns cleanly off when exiting RDR2.
[*][b]5 Player LEDs (under the touchpad):[/b] Show remaining rounds in your clip, briefly show your health whenever it changes, or sweep like police lights while wanted (configurable as Auto, Ammo, Health, or Off).
[*][b]Controller Speaker (off by default, USB only):[/b] Plays genuine in-game RDR2 sounds for holstering, the lasso, bow creaks, weapon reloads/actions, blades, weapon wheel ticks, and Dead Eye marks — and enforces a true 100% mute when disabled.
[*][b]Customizable Touchpad Gestures:[/b] Assign [i]Click, Swipe Up, Swipe Down, Swipe Left, Swipe Right[/i], and [i]Two-Finger Tap[/i] directly in the pause menu to shortcuts like Map/Camera, Whistle, Bag, Pause, Weapon Wheel, Eagle Eye, Cover, Interact, and more.
[/list]

[size=3][b]In-Game Menu (8 Languages) & PlayStation Prompts[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]63 Settings in the Pause Menu:[/b] Every option lives right inside [b]Pause > Settings > Controls > Controller > DualSense[/b], with clear descriptions and smart sub-options that hide cleanly when a feature is off.
[*][b]8 Languages Built In:[/b] Automatically matches RDR2 in [b]English, German, French, Spanish (Spain & LATAM), Italian, Portuguese, Polish, or Russian[/b] (falling back to English for other game languages).
[*][b]PlayStation Button Icons Included:[/b] Shows Rockstar's own PlayStation button prompts and controller diagram in menus and tutorials — no separate PS icon mod needed.
[/list]

[size=3][b]Experimental Bonus Features (Off by Default)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Dialogue Haptics:[/b] Feel every spoken line in cutscenes or world conversations as the speaker's own voice from the side they stand on, with adjustable [b]TV / Headphone Delay[/b] sync (Auto already accounts for Bluetooth headphones).
[*][b]Gyro Aiming:[/b] Fine tilt-to-aim adjustments while aiming with L2 (or always), factory-calibrated and drift-free.
[*][b]Microphone — Whistle & Voice Commands (USB):[/b] Whistle into the DualSense mic to call your horse (works out of the box), or say [i]"chao"[/i] to greet passersby (counts for honor) and [i]"easy"[/i] to calm your horse. Press the controller's Mute button anytime to pause/resume listening (orange Mute LED = paused).
[/list]

[size=3][b]Plug-and-Play Comfort, Wireless & Stability[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]USB or Bluetooth — Plug In Anytime:[/b] Full HD voice-coil haptics work identically over USB and Bluetooth (or switch to classic rumble motors over BT in the menu). You can even plug in or reconnect your DualSense mid-game without restarting RDR2.
[*][b]Quiet Menus & Clean Shutdown:[/b] Triggers stay completely relaxed and haptics go quiet on the title screen, in pause menus, and during loading screens. Quitting RDR2 cleanly resets the controller so lightbar and LEDs turn off properly.
[*][b]Built for Smooth Save/Load & Exit:[/b] Designed for crash-free save reloads with ScriptHookRDR2 V2 and clean game exits.
[*][b]Handy Shortcuts:[/b] Press [b]F9[/b] (or hold the PS button for 1 second) to reload [code]DualSenseRDR2.ini[/code] in-game; press [b]F10[/b] to inspect and customize the ground material under your feet.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/reddeadredemption2/mods/1472]ScriptHookRDR2 V2[/url] (recommended) or classic [url=https://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/]Script Hook RDR2[/url] + an ASI loader ([code]dinput8.dll[/code] or [code]version.dll[/code])
[*]A PlayStation DualSense (or DualSense Edge) controller via USB cable or Bluetooth (over Bluetooth everything works except the built-in speaker and microphone)
[*]Close DSX / DS4Windows and disable [b]Steam Input[/b] for RDR2 ([i]Steam > RDR2 > Properties > Controller > Disable Steam Input[/i])
[*]Remove other DualSense mods (e.g., DualSenseHaptics.asi, NodixDualSense.asi) and other PlayStation icon mods that replace [code]startup_data.rpf[/code]
[*][url=https://www.rdr2mods.com/downloads/rdr2/tools/76-lennys-mod-loader-rdr/]Lenny's Mod Loader (LML)[/url] for the in-game Settings menu and PlayStation button prompts (without LML, everything else still works via [code]DualSenseRDR2.ini[/code])
[*]For voice commands only (beta, optional): Windows' offline [b]English (US)[/b] speech recognition ([i]Settings > Time & language > Speech[/i]). The horse whistle works without it.
[*]Story mode only — move mod files out before playing Red Dead Online
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation & Updating[/b][/size]
Simply extract everything from the zip directly into your main RDR2 folder (where [code]RDR2.exe[/code] is located):
[code]DualSenseRDR2.asi
DualSenseRDR2\   (DualSenseCore.dll, weapons.json, haptics.json, translations.json, etc.)
lml\             (merges into your LML folder: lml\DualSenseRDR2 and lml\replace\...)[/code]
To update from an older version, simply copy the new files over the old ones — your [code]DualSenseRDR2.ini[/code] is preserved.
